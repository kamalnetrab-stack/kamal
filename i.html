what you want vinay. A paragraph is a unit of writing consisting of one or more sentences, each discussing a single idea or topic to create a coherent and structured piece of prose. It serves to organize thoughts and break up text for clarity, beginning with an indent and typically containing a topic sentence, supporting details, and sometimes a concluding sentence to transition to the next idea.

  • Single Idea/Topic: Each paragraph focuses on one main point, event, description, or concept.
  • Topic Sentence: The paragraph usually begins with a topic sentence that introduces the main idea.
  • Supporting Details: Body sentences provide evidence, examples, and explanations to support the topic sentence.
  • Coherence and Unity: All sentences within a paragraph are logically connected and relate to the central topic.
  • Structure: A paragraph starts on a new line, often with an indent, and continues until a new idea begins.

Parts of a Paragraph:

  1. Topic Sentence: Introduces the main idea or controlling idea of the paragraph.
  2. Body Sentences: Offer details, facts, and examples to elaborate on the topic sentence.
  3. Concluding or Bridge Sentence: Wraps up the idea and smoothly leads the reader to the next paragraph or topic.
