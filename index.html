what you want vinayA paragraph is a unit of writing consisting of one or more sentences, each discussing a single idea or topic to create a coherent and structured piece of prose. It serves to organize thoughts and break up text for clarity, beginning with an indent and typically containing a topic sentence, supporting details, and sometimes a concluding sentence to transition to the next idea. Key Characteristics of a Paragraph Single Idea/Topic: Each paragraph focuses on one main point, event, description, or concept. Topic Sentence: The paragraph usually begins with a topic sentence that introduces the main idea. Supporting Details: Body sentences provide evidence, examples, and explanations to support the topic sentence. Coherence and Unity: All sentences within a paragraph are logically connected and relate to the central topic. Structure: A paragraph starts on a new line, often with an indent, and continues until a new idea begins. Parts of a Paragraph 1. Topic Sentence: The sentence that introduces the main idea or controlling idea of the paragraph. 2. Body Sentences: These sentences offer details, facts, and examples to elaborate on the topic sentence. 3. Concluding or Bridge Sentence: A sentence that wraps up the idea and smoothly leads the reader to the next paragraph or topic.
